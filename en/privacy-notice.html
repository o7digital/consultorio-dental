Data controller and address

Consultorio Dental Satélite, under the responsibility of dental surgeon Lourdes Carrillo Rojas, located at Circuito Historiadores 44, Local 3, Ciudad Satélite, Naucalpan, Estado de México, postal code 53100, is responsible for processing the data you provide when requesting information or an appointment.

Personal data and purposes

When you contact us by phone, email or WhatsApp, we may receive your name, phone number, email address and the content of your inquiry. We use this information to respond, arrange appointments and follow up on your request. You do not need to send clinical information to request an appointment.

Health information is sensitive personal data. If your care requires medical history, test results or other clinical data, the practice must explain its purposes and obtain the appropriate consent when required. This notice covers contact through the website; it does not replace the privacy and consent documents associated with your clinical record.

Use, disclosure and retention

The purpose of contact is to address your request. Any additional use requiring your authorization must be explained and consented to separately. You may request limits on the use or disclosure of your information through the channels below. Data retention and deletion are subject to the purpose of your request and applicable legal obligations.

Your rights and how to exercise them

You may request access to, correction or cancellation of your data, or object to its processing (ARCO rights under Mexican law). You may also withdraw consent or limit its use by writing to midentista@consultoriodentalsatelite.com.mx or submitting your request at the practice address.

Include your name, a contact method for our response, the right you wish to exercise and a description of your request. To protect your information, your identity or legal representation will be verified, and only necessary documents will be requested through a channel agreed with you. A response will be sent through your specified channel within applicable legal deadlines. Cancellation and withdrawal of consent may be limited by retention obligations or other legal requirements.

External services and browsing data

This website has no registration forms or its own advertising or analytics tools. The hosting provider may process technical connection data, such as IP addresses and access logs, to provide and protect the service.

The contact section embeds Google Maps. When the map loads, Google may receive technical connection data and use cookies according to its privacy policy. You can manage cookies through your browser.

If you choose WhatsApp, Instagram or an external link, the relevant service will process your data under its own policies. This notice does not authorize data transfers for purposes unrelated to your request. When a transfer requires consent, it must be obtained beforehand, except where a legal exemption applies.

Changes and contact

Updates will be published on this page with their revision date. For privacy inquiries, use the email address above or call 55 5393 8629.

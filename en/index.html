Treatments

Solutions to help you smile
with confidence.

From prevention to full-mouth rehabilitation, we develop a clear, personalized plan for you.

01

Smile design

A cosmetic plan in harmony with your facial features.

About this treatment

We assess your teeth, gums and bite to develop a plan that may include whitening, orthodontics, restorations, veneers or implants.

Ask us on WhatsApp ↗
02

Dental implants

An option for replacing missing teeth.

About this treatment

Titanium implants are placed in the jawbone to restore function without reshaping adjacent teeth. A consultation helps determine whether they are suitable for you.

Ask us on WhatsApp ↗
03

Prevention and diagnosis

Caring for your smile starts with understanding your oral health.

About this treatment

We examine your teeth, gums, bite, jaw joint and oral tissues, using photographs and additional tests when needed. We provide guidance on brushing, flossing, fluoride and sealants.

Ask us on WhatsApp ↗
04

Oral surgery

Surgical care tailored to your needs.

About this treatment

Depending on your clinical assessment, we offer wisdom tooth extractions, implant placement, bone preservation and augmentation, bone regeneration and gum procedures.

Ask us on WhatsApp ↗
05

Orthodontics

Straighter teeth and a balanced bite.

About this treatment

We offer braces and Invisalign clear aligners. After assessing your case, we explain which treatment option best suits your needs.

Ask us on WhatsApp ↗
06

Root canal treatment

Treatment to help preserve your natural tooth.

About this treatment

We clean and seal the inside of a damaged or infected tooth to address the cause of pain and help preserve the tooth.

Ask us on WhatsApp ↗
07

Dental prosthetics

Restore function when eating, speaking and smiling.

About this treatment

Dental prosthetics can replace one or more missing teeth or restore damaged teeth. We recommend a restorative option based on your assessment.
